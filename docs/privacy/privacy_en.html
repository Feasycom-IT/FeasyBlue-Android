FeasyBlue Privacy Policy

Version V1　Updated: November 20, 2026　Effective: November 20, 2026

Shenzhen Feasycom Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") understands the importance of your personal information and is committed to protecting it. This policy explains how FeasyBlue (package name: com.feasycom.feasyblue, the "App") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information, and the rights you have.

1. What the App is

The App is a local debugging and configuration tool for Bluetooth and WiFi modules: it discovers and connects to BLE / classic Bluetooth SPP / WiFi modules and gateways, reads and writes device parameters, runs transparent-transmission and throughput tests, performs firmware over-the-air updates (OTA/DFU), and scans QR codes for device information. The App has no account registration, and offers no content publishing, community, live streaming, advertising or chat features.

Product page

The "Product" page in the App is an embedded view of our product catalogue (www.feasycom.cn), used only to show product models and specifications. Purchases are made by phone or email using the contact details on that page. The App provides no ordering, payment, order-tracking or shipping features and does not collect order or payment information. Information we do NOT collect

The App does not collect, read or upload: IMEI, IMSI, OAID, Android ID, phone number, personal name, national ID, contacts, SMS, call logs, photos/albums, microphone recordings, installed-app list, precise location, crash logs, or any device unique identifier. It contains no advertising, analytics or push SDKs.

About the clipboard: the App does not collect or upload clipboard content. The clipboard is read only when you long-press a UUID input field and choose "Paste", solely to fill that field, and the content is not stored; the App writes to the clipboard only when you tap "Copy".

3. Our "no collection before consent" commitment

Before you tap "Agree":
  • We do not initialize the Bluetooth SDK, and do not access the Bluetooth adapter or scan for devices;
  • We make no network requests (firmware download, feedback submission);
  • We do not read IMEI, IMSI, OAID, device MAC, app list or any other information (the clipboard is read only when you actively "Paste", see the section above);
  • We do not request Bluetooth, location, camera or WiFi permissions.
Only after you tap "Agree" do these features start working as described in this policy.

6. How we store and protect information

  • Location of storage: Device records, parameters, QR images and firmware files are stored only on your phone; feedback is transmitted to our server over HTTPS.
  • Retention: Local data is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data; feedback is used only to diagnose issues and deleted per company policy after handling.
  • Security: We use access control and transport encryption (HTTPS); however, no Internet environment is absolutely secure.
  • Exceptions: We may provide information where required by law or by competent authorities.

7. Sharing, transfer and public disclosure

  • Sharing: Apart from feedback you voluntarily submit (uploaded to our own feedback endpoint), we do not share your personal information with any third party.
  • Transfer: No transfer, except in the event of a merger or separation, in which case the recipient remains bound by this policy.
  • Public disclosure: None, except as required by law or with your separate consent.
  • Cross-border transfer: We do not transfer your personal information outside your country/region.

8. Auto-start and broadcast receivers

  • The App does not listen for BOOT_COMPLETED and cannot auto-start, be launched by other apps, or pull itself up in the background;
  • The App declares no static broadcast receivers in its manifest;
  • Only while you are actively scanning, the App temporarily registers system Bluetooth/network state broadcasts (to receive discovery, scan-finished and pairing-state events). The receiver is unregistered immediately when scanning stops or you leave the page, so nothing is monitored in the background.

9. Your rights

  • Access and correction: You can view device records and parameters in the App; contact us for correction.
  • Deletion: You can delete device records in the App, or clear app data/uninstall the App to delete all local data.
  • Withdraw consent: Clear app data or uninstall the App. After withdrawal we will no longer initialize the Bluetooth SDK or make network requests. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • Account deletion: The App has no accounts, so no account deletion is needed.
  • Complaints: Contact us using the details below; we will respond within 15 business days.

10. Protection of minors

The App is intended for enterprise users and technicians. If you are under 14, please read this policy with your guardian and use the App only with their consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors and will delete it promptly if discovered.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be presented in a dialog at app start to obtain your consent again, and significant changes (purposes, data types, sharing recipients) will be notified prominently. The version number is shown under the title.
